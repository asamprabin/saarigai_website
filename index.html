Case 02 · Wholesale Sweets & Dual-App Platform
JK Traders: From Excel Phone Orders to a Dual-App Wholesale Engine
JK Traders · Thoothukudi→100+ Sweet Retailers & Outlets
1

The situation

JK Traders, based in Thoothukudi, is a major wholesale seller of iconic regional sweets — including Thoothukudi Macaroons, Srivilliputhur Palkova, and Tirunelveli Halwa.

All bulk orders arrived via chaotic phone calls and WhatsApp, manually transcribed into Excel. Fresh batches with tight shelf-lives suffered from order mix-ups, stockouts, and 20+ hours of weekly admin firefighting.

2

What Saarigai built

Saarigai replaced the spreadsheet bottlenecks with a synchronized dual mobile app ecosystem:

  • Retailer E-Commerce Mobile App: Sweet shop retailers view live inventory, place bulk orders, and track shipments 24/7
  • Admin Operations Mobile App: JK Traders team approves orders, allocates fresh batches, and dispatches in 1 click
  • Live stock tracking across Macaroons, Palkova & Halwa
  • Automated digital invoices & instant dispatch manifests
  • Built & deployed in days using AI-assisted engineering
3

The result & ROI
